GST/HST Registration & Invoice Checker

Not tax or legal advice. This tool checks whether a properly formatted GST/HST number appears in text you paste — it cannot verify that a number is real, active, or actually yours. It does not check with the CRA. Confirm your actual registration status directly with the CRA or a licensed accountant.
The rule: You cannot legally charge GST or HST until you are registered and have been issued a GST/HST number. A valid number follows the pattern: 9 digits + RT + 4 digits (e.g. 123456789 RT 0001) — the same 9-digit Business Number issued by the CRA, followed by an RT program identifier.
No. 01

Before you charge tax — checklist

I have actually registered for a GST/HST account with the CRA (not just applied for a business number).
I have my GST/HST number in hand, in the format 9 digits + RT + 4 digits.
My invoice template includes this number wherever tax is charged.
I haven't already sent invoices charging tax before I had a valid number (if I have, I know I need to review and correct them).

Not financial, legal, or eligibility advice. This is a starting directory of well-known, legitimate Canadian small business support programs, with a personal tracker to log what you've researched. It does not check your eligibility, submit applications, or guarantee funding — program details, amounts, and eligibility rules change, so confirm everything on each program's official website before applying.
Start here first: the Government of Canada's own Business Benefits Finder is the official, up-to-date tool for matching your specific business to federal, provincial, and territorial programs — answer its questions honestly and write down every program it returns. The directory below is a curated set of well-established starting points, not a replacement for it.

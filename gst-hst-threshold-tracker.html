GST/HST Small Supplier Threshold Tracker

Not tax or legal advice. This tool applies the CRA's published small-supplier threshold rule to the revenue you enter. It doesn't know your actual sales records, doesn't account for every exception (e.g. specific zero-rated supplies, associated businesses), and doesn't register you for anything. Confirm your situation with a licensed accountant or the CRA directly before relying on this.
The rule: Once your total taxable revenue (before expenses) exceeds $30,000, you stop being a "small supplier" and must register for GST/HST. This is checked two ways: if a single calendar quarter alone exceeds $30,000, you become a registrant immediately, starting with the sale that put you over — you must charge GST/HST from that point and register within 29 days. If you exceed $30,000 gradually over four consecutive calendar quarters (without any single quarter alone doing it), you stop being a small supplier at the end of the month following the quarter where the rolling total crossed $30,000, and must register within 29 days of that date. Source: CRA's GST/HST registration rules for small suppliers.

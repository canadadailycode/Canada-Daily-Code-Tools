Check your explanation's readability

This uses the Flesch Reading Ease and Flesch-Kincaid Grade Level formulas — standard, published readability measures based on sentence length and syllable count. A Reading Ease score above 60 (roughly grade 8 or below) is considered easily understood by most adults; scores below 30 are considered difficult, typically requiring a university-level reader.

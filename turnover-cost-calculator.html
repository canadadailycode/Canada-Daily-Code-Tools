Employee Turnover Cost Calculator

Not financial or HR advice. This tool adds up the numbers you provide into a real turnover cost figure. It doesn't predict future turnover, know your actual payroll, or account for every cost (e.g. severance, benefits continuation) — it calculates the specific factors the video names, based on your estimates.
The calculation: Total turnover cost = replacement salary paid so far + (training hours × your hourly value) + (lost productivity hours during ramp-up × your hourly value) + (hiring/admin hours × your hourly value). Standard addition — you can verify it yourself.

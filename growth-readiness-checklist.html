Growth Readiness Checklist

Not medical, psychological, or professional advice. This is a plain business self-check for the moment you notice yourself pulling back from growth — not a mental health assessment. If pulling back is tied to something that feels bigger than business decisions, talk to a professional you trust.
Use this the moment you notice yourself pulling back — missing calls, delaying orders, avoiding the next step — right when things are actually going well.
No. 01

Exhaustion or fear? A quick honest check

Physical signs I haven't slept well, taken a break, or eaten properly in the last few days.
Timing This pulling-back started right after something went well — a big order, positive feedback, or visible growth.
Specificity It's not "everything," it's specifically the tasks that make me more visible or accountable (calls, posting, following up).
Rest test A full day off or a good night's sleep would likely fix most of this feeling.
What-if thoughts I've caught myself imagining being disappointed customers, public failure, or not living up to new expectations.

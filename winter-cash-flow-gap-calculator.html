Winter Cash Flow Gap Calculator

Not financial or accounting advice. This tool adds up the numbers you provide into a single winter cash gap figure — it doesn't predict this winter's actual costs or revenue, and it's only as accurate as your estimates. Confirm significant budget decisions with your own records and, where useful, an accountant.
The exercise: list every cost that rises for you in winter, then compare your typical winter revenue against the rest of the year. The tool adds both sides together into the real cash gap to save for before winter begins.

Not customs, legal, or financial advice. Actual US duty rates depend on your product's specific HS/HTS classification and change with trade policy — this tool does not look them up for you. Enter your own researched rate. Confirm your classification and current rate with a customs broker or official US government resource before relying on this.
The decision: when shipping to the US, you choose between DDP (Delivered Duty Paid — you collect and prepay duties, customer sees one final price) or DDU/DAP (Delivered At Place — the customer pays duties and possibly a brokerage fee at delivery, which is what causes the surprise-bill refusals the video describes). Look up your product's estimated duty rate using the US Harmonized Tariff Schedule (hts.usitc.gov) or ask a customs broker, then use it below.
